02 / One case in depth

Hineni — casting, video-first

The largest of the five, and the clearest example of how I work end to end — followed by two shorter reads, including the one that didn’t work.

Problem

Casting runs on emailed reels, spreadsheets and in-person rooms. Actors wait weeks for a read; producers drown in unsortable submissions.

What shipped

ReelRez 30-second clips in a swipe discovery feed, live audition rooms, an applicant pipeline board, scheduling and analytics — across web, iOS and Android.

My hands-on work

Architecture and front-end build, the video ingest and playback pipeline, real-time audition rooms, vendor selection, and running mobile releases end to end.

Result

Live on web and both app stores, auditions running remotely, and a repeatable release cadence across three surfaces.

System, simplified

Auth & roles — Django + DRF

Meor.care

Co-Founder & CTO
Problem

In-home aesthetic and longevity care is arranged ad hoc — opaque pricing, and no reliable view of which licensed provider is actually free when.

Shipped

A two-sided marketplace with a real booking engine: computed availability, instant confirm far ahead, broadcast and first-accept-wins close in — made race-proof in the database.

My work

Architecture, the availability and booking data model, Stripe Connect as merchant of record, and a PHI-free notification design — plus the technical side of the founding team.

Eccounting.io

Co-Founder & CTO
Problem

Sellers on Amazon, Shopify and Walmart could see revenue but not profit — fees, ad spend and returns lived in separate systems.

Shipped

Channel integrations feeding a reporting engine that produced true P&L per SKU and per channel.

Learned

The company didn’t make it. The integrations were the product, not plumbing — and I under-scoped how much reconciliation logic each new channel really cost. I scope integration work very differently now.

Wound down · 2021 — 2024
